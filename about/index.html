About the firm

Affordable, result-oriented law since 2015

Maks Law Firm is a Mississauga law firm of barristers and solicitors, founded in June 2015 by Murad Ali Khan. Its core practice is civil litigation, and it also acts in criminal, family, real estate, immigration, business, corporate, estate, personal injury and notary matters from an office at 77 City Centre Drive in Mississauga City Centre.

Our story

Built on a simple belief

Murad Ali Khan established Maks Law Firm in June 2015 with the belief that clients should receive legal services that are simultaneously affordable and result-oriented.

A legal problem can happen to anyone, and it usually arrives at the worst possible moment. The firm’s answer has been to bring a broad range of legal services under one roof, so a client facing a lawsuit, a charge, a separation, a home purchase or an immigration refusal can start with one call.

The firm’s stated vision is to grow into a full-service law firm. Today its work centres on civil litigation, criminal law, family law, real estate, immigration and appeals to the Federal Court by way of judicial review, alongside general legal services.
